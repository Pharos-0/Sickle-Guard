What this console does not show

    
    
      
  • It does not detect crop damage. The target throughout is a field-season below 80 % of the training-mean yield, on yields farmers recalled.
    • 
      
  • No YES-TECH output was used; none is public. The YES-TECH panel is a mechanism under stated assumptions, not a measurement of YES-TECH.
    • 
      
  • No payout, claim or farmer outcome was measured. Nobody has used this console in the field.
    • 
      
  • The held-out split has 62 field-seasons at 27 plots and 11 below threshold. Intervals are wide and no operating threshold is established.
    • 
      
  • SICKLE plots carry no district, so the official-records check is delta-wide and cannot say whether any plot was damaged.
